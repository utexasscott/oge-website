The School's practicum site in Wellington, New Zealand, has reached a milestone that says a great deal about how a placement matures. Two alumni of the Steve Hicks School of Social Work are now employed at the agency, and this spring two more MSSW students completed their final practicum there.

## The agency's approach

The Wellington agency provides counseling and wellness services, mainly for young adults ages 18 to 24. Its clinical work draws on solution-focused, narrative, cognitive behavioral and relational approaches. <!-- VERIFY: agency name and public description -->

For a clinical social work student, that mix is a strong fit. Narrative and solution-focused approaches ask practitioners to pay close attention to the stories clients tell about themselves and to the resources they already have. Working with young adults at a time of transition sharpens those skills quickly, and the age group is one many graduates will serve for the rest of their careers.

Starla Simmons serves as faculty liaison for the site and is the first point of contact for students with questions about the agency or the application.

## Why alumni on staff matters

A practicum site where former interns now work is more than a point of pride. It means the agency has found the School's graduates worth hiring, and it means current interns are supervised and mentored by people who know exactly what the transition from Austin to Wellington feels like. <!-- VERIFY: whether the alumni supervise or mentor current interns -->

It also demonstrates the office's student-facing message in the most concrete way possible. Global experience enriches social work practice and expands career opportunities. In Wellington, two graduates' careers took shape in the place where they trained.

## About the global practicum

Each spring, final-year MSSW students can complete their advanced practicum abroad. The Office of Global Engagement coordinates with the Office of Practicum Education and Texas Global to offer clinical and administrative and policy practice placements at sites in Chile, Mexico, New Zealand and South Africa and with UNHCR in Switzerland and Hungary.

Students interested in Wellington should contact the faculty liaison and consult the current global practicum application for timing and requirements. <!-- VERIFY: application timeline and any prerequisites for the Wellington site -->
