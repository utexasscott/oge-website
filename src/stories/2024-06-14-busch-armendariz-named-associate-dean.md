Noël Busch-Armendariz, Ph.D., LMSW, MPA, will serve as the Steve Hicks School of Social Work's next Associate Dean for Global Engagement, the School announced today.

Busch-Armendariz is a University Presidential Professor and the director of the Institute on Domestic Violence & Sexual Assault (IDVSA). She succeeds Rubén Parra-Cardona, who has served as the founding Associate Dean since the Office of Global Engagement (OGE) was created in 2023. <!-- VERIFY: effective date of the appointment -->

> Global engagement has been a part of our signature education landscape for more than 20 years.
>
> — Dean Allan Cole

## Four decades across borders

Busch-Armendariz's international experience spans more than 40 years and includes work in Albania, Dubai, England, India, Ireland, Lebanon, Scotland and South Korea. She served in the Peace Corps in Romania and has taught and supervised students in Ghana and Mexico.

She leads the School's Northern Ireland program in partnership with Corrymeela, a community in Ballycastle whose mission is to build trust and understanding. Through "Leading with Peace: Lessons from Northern Ireland," a President's Award for Global Learning program, she works with faculty colleagues Bruce Kellison and Monica Martinez and 15 students from many majors. <!-- VERIFY: program timing relative to this announcement -->

> Social workers address society's greatest challenges at home and abroad.
>
> — Noël Busch-Armendariz

## The office she will lead

OGE strengthens the School's mission by leading, supporting and coordinating its global engagement initiatives. It fosters strategic partnerships on campus and internationally, cultivates a community of international students and scholars, and supports and creates opportunities for students, faculty, staff, alumni, professionals and lifelong learners to connect with peers and institutions worldwide.

The office is a strategic priority aligned with the School's 2024–2026 plan, and its work is organized around three pillars: education abroad, research and service. Tanya Voss, LCSW, Clinical Professor, has served as Assistant Dean for Global Engagement since August 2023.

The School's international footprint predates the office by decades. It first regularly placed students abroad in 1993, with the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees in Geneva, and has since placed students on every continent except Antarctica. Faculty-led internships for MSSW students have run in 20 countries.

## What the appointment signals

Busch-Armendariz brings to the role a combination the office was built for: deep experience abroad and a research institute at home that works on some of the most difficult problems social work addresses. Her vision for the office is captured in a phrase that has become its student-facing tagline: when members of the social work community engage globally, they leave to learn and return to lead.

Questions about global research and initiatives can be directed to Busch-Armendariz at nbusch@austin.utexas.edu.
