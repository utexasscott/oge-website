Tanya Voss, LCSW, Clinical Professor, will become Assistant Dean for Global Engagement at the Steve Hicks School of Social Work effective August 15, 2023. She joins Rubén Parra-Cardona, appointed this summer as the School's first Associate Dean for Global Engagement, in leading the new Office of Global Engagement (OGE).

> International social work has such a vital role to play in our world today.
>
> — Tanya Voss, Assistant Dean for Global Engagement

## Seventeen years of field education

Voss comes to the role from field education, which she led for 17 years. Over that time she created the Community Partnership Development Committee, which has added roughly 40 new internship sites each year, and she grew the School's grant-funded programs from one to ten, representing up to $639,000 in student funding in 2022–23. She also helped raise internship support to $1.2 million.

That record matters for the new office. Global practicums depend on the same things domestic ones do: vetted agencies, trained supervisors, clear learning agreements and funding that makes participation possible. Voss has spent nearly two decades building exactly that infrastructure.

## Already global

Voss's teaching is already international. She teaches the Global Practicum Seminar, which prepares MSSW students for final practicums abroad, and she co-leads the School's May Term course in London with Professor Lauren Gulbas. That course examines the historical and contemporary contexts of the British welfare state, social work practice and institutional efforts to address social injustice, with students taking on projects with local social service agencies and living in vetted homestays. <!-- VERIFY: Voss's PUC faculty advisor role start date -->

## A new office for a long history

OGE was created this year to lead, support and coordinate the School's global engagement initiatives, to build international partnerships, to enhance education that addresses global social issues, and to foster collaboration between universities, research institutions and organizations worldwide.

The commitment it formalizes goes back decades. The School first regularly placed students internationally in 1993, with the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees in Geneva, a partnership that continues today. Since then, students have been placed on every continent except Antarctica, and faculty-led internships for MSSW students have run in 20 countries.

Current global practicum sites include Santiago, Chile; Oaxaca, Mexico; Wellington, New Zealand; Cape Town, South Africa; and UNHCR offices in Geneva and Budapest. Undergraduates also participate through Projects with Underserved Communities, a yearlong service-learning sequence run with Texas Global and the Cockrell School of Engineering. <!-- VERIFY: which practicum sites were active in 2023 -->

Voss's appointment pairs a leader with deep practicum expertise with an office whose first task is to make those opportunities more coherent, more visible and more accessible to students across the School.
