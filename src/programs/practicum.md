Learn from practitioners around the world and build your global social work skills by completing your final practicum immersed in another culture. The Office of Global Engagement coordinates with the Office of Practicum Education and UT's Texas Global to offer advanced Clinical and APP practicums each spring working with children, families, refugees, indigenous business owners, exemplary social work healthcare teams, community organizing, and counselling settings.

## Why a global practicum

Your final practicum is where the MSSW comes together: theory meets practice, and you begin to work like the professional you are becoming. Doing that work in another country adds a second layer of learning. You see how a different welfare system, culture and language shape what "help" looks like, and you return to Texas with a wider repertoire and a sharper sense of your own assumptions.

The School first placed students internationally in 1993, with UNHCR in Geneva, and has since placed students on every continent except Antarctica. Today's sites are long-standing partnerships, each with a UT faculty liaison who knows the agency, the supervisors and the city. You are never on your own: all global practicum students meet in the Global Practicum Seminar, a cohort that keeps you connected to classmates placed around the world and to the School's practicum requirements.

**For donors and partners:** global practicums are among the most transformative experiences the School offers, and they depend on gifts that offset travel and living costs. Learn how to [support global engagement](/give/).

## Eligibility & how it works

Global practicums are advanced placements for MSSW students in their final practicum, in either the Clinical or the APP concentration. Clinical placements in Santiago and Oaxaca require fluent Spanish. Because you will be abroad for a full semester, plan early and start the conversation well before your final-year placement planning begins.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><h3>Attend an information session</h3><p>OGE and the Office of Practicum Education hold sessions each year. Join the <a href="/connect/listserv/">OGE listserv</a> to hear when the next one is scheduled.</p></li>
  <li><h3>Talk with the faculty liaison</h3><p>Each site has a liaison who can describe the agencies, supervision, language expectations and day-to-day life, and help you decide whether the placement fits your learning goals.</p></li>
  <li><h3>Apply</h3><p>Submit the global practicum application through the Office of Practicum Education and complete Texas Global's education abroad requirements. Priority deadline: see the current application.</p></li>
  <li><h3>Placement confirmation</h3><p>Once the partner agency confirms your placement, you finalize travel, housing and insurance with support from OGE and Texas Global.</p></li>
  <li><h3>Global Practicum Seminar</h3><p>Enroll in the seminar, where you prepare for cross-cultural practice and, while abroad, stay connected to classmates and faculty.</p></li>
  <li><h3>Go</h3><p>Spend the spring semester at your agency, complete your final practicum requirements and return ready to lead.</p></li>
</ol>

## Funding

A global practicum costs more than a local one, and the School works to narrow that gap. The School's practicum office administers practicum funding programs, and grant-funded internship support has grown substantially in recent years. Which programs apply to a given placement changes from year to year, so start with the [Office of Practicum Education](https://socialwork.utexas.edu/academics/practicum-education/)<!-- VERIFY: practicum office URL --> and with [Texas Global's education abroad resources](https://global.utexas.edu/abroad). Current amounts and deadlines are published with each year's application rather than on this page.

## Questions?

Start with an information session or with [Tanya Voss](https://socialwork.utexas.edu/directory/tanya-voss/), who oversees global practicum placements and teaches the seminar. Have questions about a specific agency or program? Additional information is available by reaching out to the faculty liaison associated with that program.
