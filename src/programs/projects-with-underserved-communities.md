## Overview

Projects with Underserved Communities (PUC) is a yearlong service-learning sequence run jointly by Texas Global, the Cockrell School of Engineering and the School of Social Work. Teams of undergraduate engineering and social work students partner with a community abroad, spend an academic year designing a project the community has asked for, and then travel together the following summer to build it.

Since the program launched in 2010, nearly 300 Longhorns have worked alongside communities on four continents.<!-- VERIFY: launch year. Texas Global says 2010; The Utopian says 2009. --> Projects have brought water access, clean cookstoves, sanitation, accessibility improvements, health clinics, solar power and community learning centers to the places that requested them.

For social work students, PUC is a rare chance to practice community engagement at the very start of a project, when the questions are about trust, priorities and who gets a say, rather than only at the end. For engineering students, it is a lesson in the fact that a well-built thing only matters if the people it is meant for will use it. Students, as faculty member Farya Phillips has observed, "learn from the communities they serve."

## The yearlong sequence

PUC is taught as three linked courses, all open to social work and engineering undergraduates.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><h3>Fall · ES 277K Project Development</h3><p>Teams form, meet their community partner and learn the fundamentals of community engagement, needs assessment, budgeting and fundraising.</p></li>
  <li><h3>Spring · ES 277L Project Design</h3><p>Teams move from problem to plan: technical design, cultural and logistical preparation, risk planning and the public speaking needed to present the project to supporters.</p></li>
  <li><h3>May Term · ES 177L Project Implementation</h3><p>Final preparation, then travel the summer after the sequence to implement the project with the community and document the results.</p></li>
</ol>

Along the way, students build skills that employers and graduate programs notice: project management, design, budgeting, fundraising, cross-cultural collaboration, community engagement and public speaking.

## Where teams have worked

PUC teams have partnered with communities in Mexico, Peru, Ghana, Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Guatemala, Tanzania, Papua New Guinea, India, Jamaica, Greece and Thailand. In 2025–26, teams are working in Costa Rica, Greece and on two projects in India.

## Impact

- **Nearly 300 Longhorns** have taken part since the program began.
- **Four continents** have hosted PUC teams.
- **More than 18,300 people** have been served by PUC projects.
- Project types include water access, cookstoves, sanitation, accessibility, health clinics, solar power and community learning centers.

**For donors and partners:** fundraising is part of every PUC team's work, and gifts to the School's global engagement programs help ensure that cost is never the reason a student cannot join. Learn how to [support global service](/give/).

## Spotlight: Tamil Nadu, India

The School's India team, led by faculty member Farya Phillips, works with Church's Auxiliary for Social Action (CASA) in Tamil Nadu. Rather than only building something new, the team went back to evaluate what earlier projects had accomplished: a children's library opened in 2021 in Kallathupatti, a village of about 3,000 residents, and a 2015 housing project for a nomadic community.

The team produced community engagement protocols that future PUC teams can use, a service-learning documentary and a book of community stories told by the people of Kallathupatti themselves. Phillips presented the work at the President's Award for Global Learning showcase in early November 2025. It is a model of what social work brings to PUC: the discipline to ask whether a project worked, and for whom.

PUC is open to undergraduate social work and engineering students. Applications are handled by Texas Global and are accepted on a rolling basis, with a priority deadline each year in early April and final acceptances through the summer. Priority deadline: see the current application.
